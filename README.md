Analysis of national dairy production and cost data to assist producers in benchmarking their operation compared to national trends to target areas of opportunity.

Current State
In the current state the project provides trends in dairy cattle production and cost of production from 2021 to 2024. The project observes differences in trends between varying operation sizes. 
The KPIs for the project identify main costs of production, as well as the margin per hundredweight of milk and the margin per cow is also provided. The modeling portion of the projects utilizes random forest tree to predict the net margin given operating costs, allocated overhead, operation size, milk price to feed price gap, and milk per cow annually. The feature importance analysis of this model provides information on which variables have the larges impact on net margin. The project utilizes dairy industry datasets from the USDA. The four datasets currently used are: U.S. milk production and related data, U.S. dairy situation at a glance, Milk production and factors affecting supply, and Milk Cost of Production Estimates (2021 Base, By size of operation). A current limitation is that the dataset observing cost of production estimates observes only the years 2021 through 2024, older datasets observing 2005 through 2016 are available to be added into the project to provide more long term analysis of trends. Another limitation is that the project provides a broad overview of the dairy industry rather than providing specifics. Adding specifics such as the main contributers to operating cost and overhead, could prove more useful for industry managers. Another limitation is that the project as is only provides information on national averages, in future updates of the project it may prove usefull to allow users to input data about their operation to allow direct comparison to national averages.
