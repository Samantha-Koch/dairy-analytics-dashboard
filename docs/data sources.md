Customer data required:  
Production logs containing: total milk production (monthly), milk production per cow (monthly), milk composition, and bulk tank SCC.   

Health records containing: dry period per cow and SCC monthly per cow.
Financial records containing: monthly revenue from milk sales and monthly feed costs.  

Feed management system data containing information on feed consumption per cow.   

Inventory records regarding monthly milk loss due to spoilage.
