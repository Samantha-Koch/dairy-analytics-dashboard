In the United States the average herd size is around 300 head, and the average herd size has been consistently trending upward. The target buyer of this product would be mid sized operations with herds on the higher end of the range, with herd sizes of about 500 to 999 head. The product would be aimed towards producers currently utilizing spreadsheets to manage data, that want to increase their utilization of data analytics to increase profitability.   
The use of technology to better track production and operation data is becoming increasingly important in the dairy industry. Such tools include the ability to track milk production, milk composition, and feed consumption for individual cows. This product would be aimed toward operations that are beginning to implement these technologies.   
Utilization of the product would allow operators to gain a better understanding of production trends from their historical data. The product would also allow for analysis of new data streams focused on individual cow data, milk composition, feed efficiency, and health information. Analysis of the data will allow for identification of potential and current problem areas, as well as areas of strength.
